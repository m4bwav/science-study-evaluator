# Changelog: science-study-evaluator

Every change to [SKILL.md](SKILL.md), the references, the script and the companions, newest first, each with the reason. Reasons cite findings in [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md) (`R-`), lessons in [LEARNINGS.md](LEARNINGS.md) (`L-`) and test runs in [TESTS.md](TESTS.md) (`T-`). State in `evergreen.json`. Protocol: MAINTENANCE.md.

Entry shape: `### C-YYYYMMDD-n · date · one-line summary`, then `because:` (IDs or "user request"), `files:` (file and section), and a sentence on what changed. Cite section headings, not line numbers.

### C-20261114-1 · 2026-11-14 · 0.3.0: rival explanations, rescued claims, "no evidence of harm", counting hits
- because: R-20261114-1 (Sagan, The Demon-Haunted World, 1995); book rules, unverified on our own appraisals
- files: references/methodology.md §3, §7, §Changelog (version 2026-11-14.1); RESEARCH.md
- §3 asks for two rival explanations and what rules each out before the verdict. §7 grades an after-the-fact excuse for a null as spin, treats "no evidence of harm" reported as "safe" as absence of evidence relabelled, and asks for a systematic review behind a list of agreeing studies. Not adopted: Occam's razor and the authority point (no checkable step; §6 already covers paid promoters).

### C-20261113-1 · 2026-11-13 · 0.2.0: scope ladder, self-objection, warrant tests, quantity words, reread before misattribution
- because: R-20261113-1 (Booth, Colomb and Williams, The Craft of Research, 3rd ed., 2008); book rules, unverified on our own appraisals beyond the two-case behaviour check in TESTS.md T-20261113-1
- files: references/methodology.md §1, §3, §5, §7, §Changelog (version 2026-11-13.1); SKILL.md Step 3; references/explaining.md Arguing against your own verdict
- Spin is now graded per hop on a three-rung scope ladder, and a concession quoted as the finding or a claim stripped of its limiting condition is named spin. A design with no branch gets the five warrant tests. Unexplained quantity words and false precision are flags. Every review states the strongest objection to its own verdict and answers it or attaches a condition. A misattribution verdict waits on a reread of the cited passage. Not adopted: the book's reliability signs (peer review, press, citation counts), weaker than §1 and §2.

### C-20261104-1 · 2026-11-04 · Methodology: comparator per endpoint, blood as a clearance proxy, preprint diff, unreachable outlet text
- because: gaps found appraising a paper on nitroglycerin plus vibration (Tuunanen 2026, Nature Communications); author request to record the lessons; L-017
- files: references/methodology.md §3, §4, §7, §Changelog; references/fetch-routes.md Known blocks; LEARNINGS.md L-017
- A randomized label hid that the headline biomarker was measured around one arm, and the checklist had no check for that per endpoint. It also had no branch for reading blood levels as brain clearance, no preprint-to-journal comparison, and no rule for grading coverage whose text is walled off.

### C-20261011-2 · 2026-10-11 · Methodology v8: n-of-1 self-tracking branch; YouTube fetch route
- because: L-012; gap found appraising a health video that read one person's 26 home metabolomics tests against two published cohort studies
- files: references/methodology.md §3, §Changelog (version 2026-10-11.1); references/fetch-routes.md Known blocks; LEARNINGS.md L-012
- The checklist had no branch for personal lab data read against borrowed population cut-offs. In the case that exposed it, all 26 whole-blood microsample tryptophan values (19-44 µM) were graded against a plasma mortality curve whose median was 67 µM, a three-year decline about 20 times the cited aging slope was called aging, and a diet screen of an unstated number of inputs had no multiplicity correction or time adjustment. The new branch covers matrix and units, time as the confounder, the input count, home-made ratio indices and a presenter who sells the measurement. fetch-routes.md gains the YouTube route, since the citations were only on the slides.

### C-20261011-1 · 2026-10-11 · 0.1.0: first public release
- because: user request (publish the skill for anyone to install, modernised); L-002, L-009
- files: everything
- The skill had run privately since 2026-08-31 on one machine and one app, with its checklist at methodology v7. This release makes it portable. SKILL.md drops machine paths and app-specific tools and works in any agent that reads skills; appraisals and the verdict log moved to a user folder (`SSE_HOME`, default `~/.science-study-evaluator/`) with an optional `reader.md` describing who reads the reviews. The steps were reorganised into source, appraise, write, keep. The retrieval lessons became `references/fetch-routes.md`, and the glossary and analogies `references/explaining.md`. `paper_meta.py` was rewritten: argparse, `--json`, PMID and PMCID input and DOIs pulled out of publisher URLs, contact email from `--email` or `SSE_EMAIL` instead of a hard-coded address (Unpaywall is skipped without one), NCBI tool, email and API-key parameters, UTF-8 output on Windows without an environment variable, Crossref `updated-by` notices including Retraction Watch, PubMed retraction and erratum links, an INTEGRITY NOTICE in NEXT, and `--fulltext DIR`, which saves the Europe PMC article as XML and as greppable text. Added offline unit tests, CI on three operating systems, and eval cases.
- History before publication (private ids): methodology v1 2026-08-31 (baseline: CONSORT 2025, ROBINS-I v2, GRADE Book, paper-mill tools); v2 2026-09-02 (qualitative branch, sample recycling, retracted load-bearing citations); v3 2026-09-15 (composite exposures, observational-regression branch, attrition, omission as spin, publication-status drift); v4 2026-09-16 (single-arm pre/post branch and hold-out outcome, recency mirror check); v5 2026-09-28 (umbrella reviews, sibling comparisons and negative-control exposures, "not linked" as spin); v6 2026-09-30 (equivalence and non-inferiority margins, sponsored-trial comparators, Retraction Watch via Crossref); v7 2026-10-01 (coarse controls, ability exposures, single-item outcomes, the hop that added the claim). The methodology's own Changelog section has the detail.
